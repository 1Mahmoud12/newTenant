# Privacy Policy for Rova Star

**Last updated:** February 21, 2025

---

This Privacy Policy describes how **Rova Star** (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application on **Google Play** (Android) and the **Apple App Store** (iOS).

By using Rova Star, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information:** Email address, phone number, name, and other details you give when you register or sign in (including via Firebase Authentication).
- **Profile and preferences:** Data you add to your profile or settings within the app.
- **Content you submit:** Any text, images, or other content you upload or submit through the app (e.g., via image picker or forms).

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers (e.g., Android ID), and app version (via packages such as `device_info_plus`, `package_info_plus`).
- **Usage data:** How you use the app (e.g., features used, screens viewed) to improve the service and fix issues.
- **Connectivity data:** Network and connectivity status (e.g., via `connectivity_plus`) to provide a stable experience.

### 1.3 Location Information

- **Location data:** With your permission, we may collect your device’s location (via `location`, `geocoding`, and map-related features) to provide location-based features, such as maps and address-related functionality.

### 1.4 Push Notifications

- **Push notification data:** We may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (and similar services) to send you push notifications. This can involve device tokens and notification preferences.

### 1.5 Biometric Data (Optional)

- **Biometric authentication:** If you choose to use fingerprint or face recognition (e.g., via `local_auth`), authentication is processed on your device. We do not store your actual biometric data on our servers.

### 1.6 Other Technical Data

- **In-app browsing:** If you open web pages inside the app (e.g., in-app browser or payment flows), we may receive data related to those sessions as needed for the feature (e.g., completing a payment).
- **Screenshots:** If the app uses screenshot functionality, it is used only as described in the app (e.g., for support or sharing within the app) and not for undisclosed tracking.

We do not sell your personal information.

Depending on your region (e.g., EU, UK, California), you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Delete** your data or account.
- **Object or restrict** certain processing.
- **Data portability** (receive your data in a portable format where applicable).
